Mantrams for Mindfulness and Healing

Chapter 11 The Rider from the Secret Place

Come forth O Mighty One

Come forth O Mighty One

[Verse 1, Intro]
Let the Lords of Liberation issue forth.
Let Them bring succour to the sons of men.

[Chorus]
Let the Rider from the Secret Place come forth,
And coming, save.
Come forth, O Mighty One.

[Verse 2]
Let the souls of men awaken to the Light,
And may they stand with massed intent.
Let the fiat of the Lord go forth:
The end of woe has come!

[Chorus]
Let the Rider from the Secret Place come forth,
And coming, save.
Come forth, O Mighty One.

[Verse 3]
Come forth, O Mighty One.
The hour of service of the Saving Force has now arrived.
Let it be spread abroad, O Mighty One.

[Chorus]
Let the Rider from the Secret Place come forth,
And coming, save.
Come forth, O Mighty One.

[Verse 4]
Let Light and Love and Power and Death
Fulfil the purpose of the Coming One.
The WILL to save is here.

[Chorus]
Let the Rider from the Secret Place come forth,
And coming, save.
Come forth, O Mighty One.

[Verse 5]
The LOVE to carry forth the work is widely spread abroad.
The ACTIVE AID of all who know the truth is also here.

[Final Chorus (crescendo, repeat)]
Come forth, O Mighty One and blend these three.
Construct a great defending wall.
The rule of evil now must end.

[Outro (gentle, resolute)]
The rule of evil now must end.
Let the Rider from the Secret Place come forth,
And coming, save.
Come forth, O Mighty One.

The 1940 Stanza of the Great Invocation, adapted from the book The Externalisation of the Hierarchy published by the Lucis Trust. Musicalized by José Becerra.

THE GREAT INVOCATIONS

1935
Let the Forces of Light bring illumination to mankind.
Let the Spirit of Peace be spread abroad.
May men of goodwill everywhere meet in a spirit of cooperation.

May forgiveness on the part of all men be the keynote at this time.
Let power attend the efforts of the Great Ones.
So let it be, and help us to do our part. — 1935

1940
Let the Lords of Liberation issue forth.
Let Them bring succour to the sons of men.
Let the Rider from the Secret Place come forth,
And coming, save.
Come forth, O Mighty One.

Let the souls of men awaken to the Light,
And may they stand with massed intent.
Let the fiat of the Lord go forth:
The end of woe has come!
Come forth, O Mighty One.
The hour of service of the Saving Force has now arrived.
Let it be spread abroad, O Mighty One.

Let Light and Love and Power and Death
Fulfil the purpose of the Coming One.
The WILL to save is here.
The LOVE to carry forth the work is widely spread abroad.
The ACTIVE AID of all who know the truth is also here.
Come forth, O Mighty One and blend these three.
Construct a great defending wall.
The rule of evil now must end. — 1940

1945
From the point of Light within the Mind of God
Let light stream forth into the minds of men.
Let Light descend on Earth.

From the point of Love within the Heart of God
Let love stream forth into the hearts of men.
May Christ return to Earth.

From the centre where the Will of God is known
Let purpose guide the little wills of men—
The purpose which the Masters know and serve.

From the centre which we call the race of men
Let the Plan of Love and Light work out.
And may it seal the door where evil dwells.

Let Light and Love and Power restore the Plan on Earth. — 1945
